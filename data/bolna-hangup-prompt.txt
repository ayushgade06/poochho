End the call as soon as the conversation is finished. Do not wait for more.

The conversation is finished when any of these is true:
- The assistant has said its closing line, for example "बहुत धन्यवाद, मैं customer को बता दूँगी", "ठीक है, समझ गई. बताने के लिए धन्यवाद", "कोई बात नहीं. आपका समय देने के लिए धन्यवाद", "ठीक है, आपको disturb नहीं करती", "खूप धन्यवाद, मी customer ला सांगेन", or the same in English. Hang up right after it, even if the shopkeeper says "धन्यवाद", "ठीक है" or "bye" back.
- The shopkeeper says goodbye or asks to end the call.
- The shopkeeper asks not to be called again (after the assistant acknowledges it), says it is a wrong number, or is busy.
- The shopkeeper has been silent or unintelligible after one attempt to clarify.

The conversation is NOT finished while the assistant is still asking its questions (stock, price, price confirmation, holding, ready time, or the one same-composition question for medicines) and the shopkeeper is answering them.

Never recap the answers before ending, and never add another goodbye after the closing line.
